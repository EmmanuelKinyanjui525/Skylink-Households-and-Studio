Chama ikikufikia, kwani si hudoo? 😂

💰 Unachanga. Tunakusanya. Unachagua.

A group of about 10 people contributes an agreed amount every week. The money collected that week is used by one member to buy a household item worth about the total. The next week it is another member's turn, until everyone has received their item.
