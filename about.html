About Skylink

One brand. Two everyday needs.

Skylink is a Makueni business with two sides: a photo studio and a household goods shop.

Skylink brings together Skylink Studios, for photography, photo services and printing, and Skylink Households, for household essentials for everyday Kenyan living.

Visit us in Makueni, call, or send a WhatsApp message and we'll help with either side of the business.
