About the Studio

Capturing Moments. Creating Memories.

At Skylink Studios, we believe every moment has a story worth remembering. We capture authentic, beautiful and meaningful moments through professional photography, creating images that you can treasure for years to come.

We offer professional photography in a simple, affordable way — from passport and ID photos to portraits, family sessions and full event coverage, with printing and editing to finish the job.

Skylink Studios is one half of Skylink. The other half is Skylink Households, our household goods shop, in the same Makueni premises.
